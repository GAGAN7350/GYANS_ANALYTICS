Problem
Statement

Commercial Business Intelligence (BI) platforms such as Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, and Zoho Analytics impose steep recurring subscription fees, strict user-licensing restrictions, and mandatory cloud data custody.

Organizations, academic institutions, and privacy-conscious enterprises often handle sensitive proprietary data that cannot be entrusted to third-party clouds. Existing open-source alternatives are often fragmented, difficult to deploy, and lack intuitive drag-and-drop dashboard design, self-contained ETL pipelines, or lightweight zero-cost infrastructure.

Our
Solution

GYANS Analytics is an open-source, self-hosted BI platform that enables users to connect data sources, clean and transform datasets, and construct interactive dashboards via an intuitive drag-and-drop canvas.

The system eliminates SaaS subscription overheads, maintains absolute on-premise data sovereignty, and delivers fast, real-time visual analytics for modern teams.
